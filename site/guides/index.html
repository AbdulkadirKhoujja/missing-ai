What is Missing AI?

Missing AI is a free, open-source requirements comparison tool. The browser checker flags wording-based candidate omissions in AI answers; optional ChatGPT and Claude custom-MCP tools enable host-assisted semantic reviews with mechanical quote/reference validation. It does not establish real-world implementation, legal compliance or factual truth.

Does it check facts or grade work?

No. It compares an answer's claims with supplied requirements, not the world's facts. It cannot grade assignments, certify code, or audit a deployed service. The person using it remains responsible for checking source meaning and real outcomes.
